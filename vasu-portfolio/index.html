before you ask…

Q.01 so what exactly do you do?

a lot of things. mostly, i make websites work for your business. websites, web apps, games, stores, panels — if it opens in a browser, i'm your guy.

Q.02 who have you worked with?

startups, small businesses and people with one wild idea and a deadline. not to toot my own horn (okay fine, i am) but my work is live all over the internet — start with the four projects above.

Q.03 why should we hire you?

because i can do everything those 10 ai tools and 10 separate hires claim they can do — design, code, animation, deployment — and i'll do it with taste.

Q.04 what do you look for in a project?

people who care. folks who want to build things that matter, not just tick boxes. if you want a yes-person who 'just' follows orders, i'm not your person. i also can't work with people who are allergic to fun.

Q.05 how fast do you deliver?

how fast do you need it? most of my work is done in days, not weeks. i don't believe in overthinking things into oblivion. i move quick, i iterate, i deliver.

Q.06 what if i don't like your work?

then we fix it. i'm not precious about my work — i'm precious about getting it right. i'm easily excited and easily bored. as dangerous as gasoline parked next to scented candles.
