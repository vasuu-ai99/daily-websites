Founded in Bucharest by Bogdan and Diana Apalaghiei, Vings18 brings together years of experience across advertising, branding and digital.

HOW WE STARTED

Two '90s kids, one shared itch to make better brands.

After years working for others, we changed course in early 2024 and decided to put all that experience into a dream of our own. What started with some recommendations and a few familiar faces quickly grew into something of its own. A studio where we could question the brief, really look into the details and make the kind of work that makes us smile.

NOW WE'RE HERE

Less size, more scope

We have grown, but not into a maze of departments. Instead, Vings18 is a three-person core team, supported by top notch collaborators whenever a project demands it. It means we can stay involved, move quickly and build the right team around each situation. We do not chase work for the sake of filling a portfolio. We like projects with a real challenge and people who care about getting it right.

THE OFFICE RULES

01

THE BRIEF IS NOT SET IN STONE

A brief is a starting point, not a set of commandments. We'll challenge it if we think there's a better way to get where we need to go.

02

GOOD IDEAS NEED SPACE

We don't believe in rushing to the first solution that kind of works. Give an idea enough room to breathe, and it might just be the one.

03

PRETTY IS NOT A STRATEGY

We love beautiful things. But looking good is only part of the job. First, we search for meaning. Everything we put out there needs to have a reason to be.

04

IF IT CAN BE SIMPLE, SIMPLIFY

Complexity for complexity's sake is not cool, actually. And people's attention span is at an ATL. If your brand can say more with less, we'll make it happen.

CAPABILITIES — WE'VE GOT RANGE

HEYGLUCO

Health & Wellness

The Context

HeyGluco was born from a family's own experience with type 1 diabetes. After their daughter Ecaterina was diagnosed at seven, they found themselves navigating a new reality filled with medical information, calculations, uncertainty and a lot of fear. What they needed most in those first months wasn't more information, but a clearer, more human way to make sense of it. They decided to create the resource they wished they had from day one: a place where families could learn, find practical tools and feel supported by people who had already been through the same experience. Our challenge was to turn that deeply personal idea into a brand that could talk about a serious subject with clarity and empathy, without feeling clinical or intimidating. Project year - 2026

The Solution

We built HeyGluco around the idea of making diabetes easier to understand and less frightening, especially for children. At the centre of the identity is Gluco, a small dragon designed as a familiar companion through a world of sensors, insulin, glucose levels and new routines. Every detail has a purpose. His three coloured spikes reference glucose levels, while the scarf is inspired by the international symbol of diabetes. Around him, we created a cast of characters based on everyday diabetes tools, turning unfamiliar medical objects into something children can recognise and relate to. A second, more narrative illustration style helps tell the family's real story with more emotion and nuance. A warm, colourful palette moves away from the cold visual codes of healthcare, while typography and a clear editorial system make complex information easier to navigate. Together, they give HeyGluco a visual language that can educate without overwhelming and support without talking down.
